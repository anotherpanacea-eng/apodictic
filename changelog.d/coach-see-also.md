### Coach — Learning Mode see-also links

Each syllabus lesson now names two or three neighbouring lessons in a See also column, with a short reason for each link, and the Teach step closes by naming them and offering the lesson's deeper reading. The writer may take any See also lesson next. Deeper reading now lists the writer guide in `docs/writer-guides/` first where one exists, then the research brief (AP-LRN-2, `docs/coach-lesson-see-also.md`).
