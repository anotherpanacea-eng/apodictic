### Coach — brain breaks

The coach can offer a brain break: one prompt that exercises a single craft concept on material the writer invents on the spot, away from the manuscript. It has its own firewall test 1 (concept-grounded) and keeps tests 2 to 5. It is offered beside the usual intervention for avoidance blocks, execution-gap blocks and perfectionism, as one choice and never a list, and nothing the writer writes for it is stored. A state-less `/coach stuck` run may offer one. A Learning Mode exercise no longer needs a named passage: the coach asks for a paragraph of the writer's own prose first, accepts a public-domain passage only when its exact text and public-domain basis are in the session, and otherwise gives a brain break on the lesson's concept. Depletion and feedback saturation remain no-prompt zones (AP-LRN-3, `docs/coach-brain-breaks.md`).
