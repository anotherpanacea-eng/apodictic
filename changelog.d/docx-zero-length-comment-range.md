### Annotated .docx: comments on a blank line open before they close

A comment anchored on a blank line (a document note when the manuscript's first line is blank, or a
line range on an empty line) used to come out as range end, reference, then range start, which Word
may misplace or drop. A zero-length range now opens and closes at its point, between the ranges that
close there and the ones that open there.
