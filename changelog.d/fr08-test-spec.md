### Evals

FR-08: a draft test specification for conflicting-audit repair.
- **Cases:** six synthetic families built on real audit pairs (compression vs stakes, scene-turn vs emotional-craft, and others). Two are competing-obligation cases where abstaining is correct. Each has a resolved-variant control.
- **Procedure:** confirm the conflict by actually running the audits, ask with keys withheld, splice, re-audit, and have two readers from other vendors inspect.
- **Rubric and size:** a per-field rubric with no composite score, completion criteria, and a sizing block.

For owner review; nothing is constructed or run.
