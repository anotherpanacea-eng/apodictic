## Recommendation

Lean, with one narrow representation change. Design principle: *a passage's communicative work
determines which questions are relevant; its actual commitments determine what it remains
answerable for.*

1. **AT table:** keep AT1, AT2, AT5. AT3 burdens scale to the decision actually asked for (not
   every exhortation is a policy proposal). AT4 extended to recognition ("here is what I
   experienced; recognize or consider it"); interpretive and representative burdens apply only
   where undertaken. New **AT6 Relational / constitutive** ("recognize or renew who we are, how
   we stand toward one another, or what we undertake"), writer-confirmed; covers constitutive,
   commemorative (epideictic), and performative (pledge, dedication, acknowledgment) work.
2. **Finding rules:** applicability precedes findings (an inapplicable diagnostic yields no
   finding, not a Could-Fix); commitments are checked throughout; positive work is assessed for
   confirmed non-claim undertakings; faithful representation, rebuttal, alternatives, and frame
   refusal are separate obligations; high stakes intensify scrutiny of applicable commitments
   without turning every passage into a literal assertion.
3. **Narrow schema amendment ("shared D"):** permit no global C0, zero or more local claim
   chains, no global warrant verdict when there is no global inference, conditional objection
   requirements, and severity grounded in failure of the confirmed undertaking or an actual
   commitment. Consumers needing a global ladder accept the claim subset or decline; never
   fabricate C0, antitheses, edges, or WARRANTED.
4. **Evaluation:** matched-text battery showing category errors avoided and unchanged defects
   still caught.

**From PR #288, retain:** purpose/operation/commitment distinctions, applicability before
severity, local integrity checks, norm provenance, matched fixtures, consumer compatibility.
**Defer:** persisted purpose/operation records, cross-linking, profile state machinery, seven
profiles. Also adjust Dialectical Clarity's scope statement, which distances itself from
rhetoric coaching.

## Combining modes

The unit is the operation and its commitments, not an exclusive segment label. Seams to inspect:
personal account → collective claim; shared identity → action; narrative sequence →
explanation; shared value → specific prescription; analogy or lens → assertion; genealogy →
evaluation; invitation → demand; satirical persona → factual allegation. The defect is an
undisclosed change in what the reader is asked to accept, not a mode change itself. Repair notes
should name what the fix could cost (pacing, intimacy, irony).

## Recognition

Propose operations provisionally with an anchor, a plain description, and an alternative
reading; confirm in ordinary language, never by theory name. Signals are evidence, not verdicts.
Confirmation selects the task; it never exempts an actual assertion. Unresolved interpretation
yields conditional findings, not locked defects.

## Value ranking (product judgment)

1. Witness and narrative. 2. Constitutive, commemorative, performative. 3. Values, presence,
dissociation, jeremiad. 4. Enthymeme, exemplum, analogy, frame disputes. 5. Invitational and
agonistic. 6. Satire and genealogy. Cultural and performance-context safeguards ship in the
first release regardless.

## Guardrails

No form exempts an actual commitment. Testimony is evidence, not proof of universality. Author
confirmation cannot override what the text does. No inferring bad faith from labels or cues.
Normative basis of each finding stays visible. The firewall does not detect deception; output
states what was assessed and what remains unverified.

## The critique

Real but narrower than "liberal-rationalist": the engine's representation privileges an
evaluable conclusion, and a gentler treatment of a missing ladder is still wrong when no ladder
was undertaken. AT1/AT4/AT5, recoverable warrants, and narrative schemes are substantive
accommodations. Proposed documentation position: *the engine applies claim-support standards
where the text undertakes claim-support work, supports a bounded set of other communicative
undertakings, and states the basis and limits of its diagnoses.*

## Test-text expectations (summary)

- **Douglass 1852:** mixed mode; trace commemoration → indictment → possibility; later
  constitutional reasoning stays assessable.
- **Truth 1851:** treat Robinson (1851) and Gage (1863) as different witnesses; the famous
  refrain is Gage's; embodied counterexample is not a population sample.
- **Gettysburg (Nicolay copy):** dedication → undertaking; no strategy comparison.
- **Seneca Falls Declaration:** constitutive and heavily assertive; grievance repetition
  accumulates; factual claims persist.
- **Chief Joseph (Wood 1893 rendering):** mediated text (Venn); lament needs no alternatives
  analysis; not a specimen of a generalized style.
- **Constructed pair:** S0 solidarity greeting draws no thesis/objection/policy defect; S1 adds
  "will reduce neighborhood evictions by 40% within one year," flagged for causal, quantified,
  and time-horizon support. Neither fits the current schema; neither needs seven profiles.
