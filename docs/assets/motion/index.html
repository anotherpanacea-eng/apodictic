APODICTIC: two commands in thirty seconds each

Silent clips with the captions drawn into the picture. The caption text is listed under each clip. Pages in the clips show grey bars only, never real manuscript text.

/start: three questions pick your lane

  1. Type /start
  2. What do you have?
  3. What do you want?
  4. Anything that changes how we work?
  5. The answers pick your lane. Neither lane rewrites your prose.

Step through the drawn diagram

/adjudicate: you decide every claim

  1. Your argument, broken into claims
  2. You decide every claim and link.
  3. Approve it.
  4. Revise it in your own words. It comes back as a new claim.
  5. Reject it, and it stays out.
