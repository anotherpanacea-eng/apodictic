# Editorial counterfactual construction pilot - protocol 2

Status: prospective, unregistered synthetic development construction. This is
not a production-engine test, a frontier comparison, a human-licensed benchmark,
or a ground-truth promotion. Four proposed families; three variants per family.
Every family and descendant is DEV. No evaluated holdout is claimed.

## Freeze and custody
Freeze this protocol, neutral reading prompt and updating prompt before prose
generation. Preserve original authoring records and all versioned inputs.
Freeze the complete candidate set, exact dispatch prompts and private condition
map before any reading. Hash UTF-8 bytes with SHA-256, record byte lengths and
UTC timestamps. Missing artifacts and unavailable telemetry are explicit.
Private keys, condition maps, authorship and responses remain outside Git.
Public reports contain only protocol, aggregate results and artifact hashes.
Future reservation: two new families, neither based on these plots nor these
specific defect mechanisms, must be authored and frozen before any future prompt
tuning starts. There is no existing reserved holdout; this rule is prospective.
No prompt tuning in this pilot. Stop at four original families and at most one
immutable reconstruction revision per family; no repeated polishing to obtain
a desired reviewer answer. Report rejected and ambiguous cases too.

## Construction
Each original author receives only this protocol and a distinct high-level brief.
Write original synthetic prose, 250-450 words, sufficient to assess the proposed
material defect without missing external context. Privately record intended
effect, 2-3 concrete craft features, substantive commitments, one proposed defect,
its practical consequence, exact evidence, a plausible competing reading, and
permissible repair scope. Do not imitate a named living writer. Neither deliberate
craft nor character unreliability can be declared an error merely for being
unusual. Avoid a naked arithmetic/continuity puzzle already covered by FR-05.

A different author produces a minimally relevant repair with a change ledger.
A separate author produces a comparably noticeable, plausible editorial change
that leaves the target defect intact. Each receives the original and its private
construction brief, but neither receives the other's text. Record actual word
and character edit magnitudes; do not pretend magnitude matching proves equal
salience. Material unmatched edits restrict causal claims. Do not label variants
in model-facing prose; each packet has an opaque identifier and the same prompt.

## Independent construction reading
One fresh context per packet, fork_turns=none. Only common prompt plus selected
prose is dispatched. No siblings, target labels, keys, author briefs, earlier
readings, file access or external sources. A reader may report no material
criticism. This is instruction-level isolation on a shared host, not a filesystem
sandbox or proof against memorization. A dispatcher record binds exact prompt
and response bytes and actual tool dispatch settings; unavailable served-model,
token, price and reasoning telemetry is null, not an inferred identity or zero.
Construction readers are not a model benchmark or production APODICTIC run.

## Two-stage family adjudication
One independent fresh family reviewer first reads shuffled, neutrally labelled
variants without keys. It records supported defects, alternative readings,
strengths and changes without guessing assigned conditions. Seal this response
before showing the private hypotheses and author ledgers. The same reviewer then
rules on materiality, whether repair removes the target, whether control retains
it, whether commitments and craft survive, and consequential confounds. Inspect
packet readings only after its blind family judgment is sealed. Additional valid
criticisms are allowed; disagreement with the key is not automatically error.
Only this reviewed family judgment and the documented author fold determine a
construction disposition; model agreement is not a license from a human panel.

## Rubric - separate fields, never a single total
- Diagnosis: 0 absent/unsupported target, 1 relevant but incomplete, 2 materially
  correct with evidence. Record misses separately from unsupported claims. This
  field is only evaluable for variants where independent adjudication confirms
  the target remains. Target-absent/repaired variants receive N/A; unsupported
  persistence is considered under unwarranted intervention, not a diagnosis miss.
- Unwarranted intervention: none / consequential unsupported intervention /
  disputed. A valid unanticipated concern does not count against the reader.
- Conservation: pass / fail / disputed for preservation of prespecified
  substantive commitments and concrete craft features; stylistic taste alone
  cannot fail conservation.
- Relevant updating (if later evaluated): 0 invalidated claim retained, 1 partial
  narrowing with unsupported residue, 2 explicit withdrawal or precise narrowing.
- Control updating (if later evaluated): 0 abandons still-supported claim,
  1 unclear, 2 justified retention that accurately recognizes what changed.
- Do not score withdrawal if the frozen initial reading did not make that claim:
  record not-evaluable and its denominator. A newly noticed claim is different.

## Dispositions and stopping
RETAIN-CANDIDATE only if blind/keyed adjudication supports a material target,
repair resolution, control retention and craft conservation, with no material
confound. DISPUTED for a reasonable competing reading or unresolved interpretation.
REWORK for an addressable construction confound (one immutable revision allowed).
REJECT for dependence on missing context, arbitrary taste, failed preservation,
variant leakage or no useful controlled distinction. The disposition covers the
whole family; three related packets are not three independent findings.
Retained means suitable for further licensing, not proven difficulty or accuracy.
Record proposed, generated, screened, retained, disputed, rejected, revised,
incomplete and held-out counts from actual artifacts. Zero outputs is not success.

## Updating boundary
Independent readings of siblings cannot establish withdrawal. A later updating
exercise must clone the same frozen baseline passage and reading into two fresh
continuations: one with repair, one with control. Neither continuation sees its
sibling or the intended outcome. Use the frozen updating prompt. This pilot
constructs the packets and records whether a baseline target exists; it does not
run that separate evaluation or infer its result. Family keys stay scorer-side.
