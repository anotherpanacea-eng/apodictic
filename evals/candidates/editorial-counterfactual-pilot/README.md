# Editorial counterfactual development pilot

Four original synthetic families produced twelve passages: four originals,
four separately authored repairs, and four unrelated-edit controls. Twelve
fresh-context packet readings and four two-stage family adjudications are
complete. **All four families are DISPUTED; zero are retained for binary
scoring.** This is a completed construction screen, not a completed benchmark.

The principal construction finding is a distinction between a represented
character's reasoning error and an editorial defect. The former may serve a
plausible artistic purpose. Correcting it can remove irony even when the named
voice features survive. Private author intent alone did not settle the competing
readings. The unrelated edits were also smaller or semantically less consequential
than the repairs; the batch cannot isolate relevance from change magnitude,
explicitness, changed policy or changed characterization.

## Protocol and evidence

[Protocol 2](PROTOCOL.md) incorporates independent method review before prose
generation. The initial protocol remains preserved locally; a prospective
amendment makes diagnosis not applicable on target-absent repairs. The
[neutral reading prompt](reading-prompt.txt) allows abstention. The
[updating prompt](updating-prompt.txt) is prepared but was **not run**.

Four independent original authors created the prose. Separate repair and control
authors worked across the families without seeing each other's variants. Every
packet reader received only its selected passage and the same neutral prompt,
with no keys or siblings and no tools. Family reviewers first compared neutral
variants without keys, saved their judgments, and received hypotheses and packet
readings only after those judgments were frozen. All four blind judgments stayed
unchanged after key reveal. The final fold accepts their disputed dispositions.
One adjudicator's conditional numeric scores were excluded by a preserved
clarification; no admitted numeric diagnosis remains.

All current families and descendants are development material. No untouched
holdout, model comparison, production-engine run, paired updating run, or human
licensing occurred. Three baseline readings made the proposed target criticism;
one expressly treated the represented error as purposeful characterization and
recommended no intervention. That last response is not counted as a miss.
Independent sibling readings do not show withdrawal or justified retention.

Twelve distinct prose bodies span 338-377 words; exact within-batch duplication
was checked, not corpus-wide originality or decontamination. All eight mutation
ledgers reproduce their final passages exactly.

## Verification and next use

All protocol, original, candidate and response bindings verify; all four blind
family manifests still match after keyed adjudication. Five focused local custody
checks pass. APODICTIC's combined gate passed 81 of 83 validator self-tests and
subsequent canonical checks. The two failures, contract-hash and contract-check,
reproduce on unchanged base because the local Windows Git Bash lacks `shasum`.
No full-green or hosted-CI clearance is claimed; runtime and validator sources
are unchanged.

Keep these families as unscored editorial-discussion material. The next bounded
construction should supply a non-spoiling writer brief stating the intended genre
and outcome, select a defect independent of character unreliability, and author a
comparably substantive control. Freeze that redesign prospectively. Human
adjudication remains necessary before any ground-truth promotion; do not convert
a disputed interpretation into a wrong answer or quietly label these cases
held-out evidence.
