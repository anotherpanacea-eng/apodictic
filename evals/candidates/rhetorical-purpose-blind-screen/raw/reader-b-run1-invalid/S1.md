The confirmed purpose is a solidarity greeting for residents at a fictional Alder Court gathering. W1–W2 perform that purpose through welcome and a call for mutual support. W3 also commits the passage to a specific causal outcome. The stated purpose does not erase that sentence’s commitment.

**Purpose, operation and commitments**

| Anchor | Apparent passage operation | Actual commitment |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Welcomes residents across differences in identity and length of residence. | Offers universal belonging **in this room tonight**. It does not promise continued housing, legal rights or services. |
| W2 | Frames displacement as a shared loss and asks for interpersonal solidarity. | Makes a universal evaluative claim—“all of us are diminished”—and directs residents to stand with a family and communicate that they are not alone. |
| W3 | Gives the gathering an assured, measurable future consequence. | Predicts that this gathering will cause a 40% reduction in neighborhood evictions within one year. It states no mechanism, comparison baseline or measurement definition. |

The commitment inventory is therefore:

- **Factual:** W3 predicts a future eviction outcome. W1 offers belonging rather than establishing independently verified inclusion. W2 does not document an actual displacement or identify a particular affected family.
- **Causal:** W3 attributes the reduction to the gathering. W2’s shared diminishment may be an ethical judgment or a claim about collective consequences; its mechanism remains unspecified.
- **Quantified:** W3 supplies a percentage and deadline. “Reduce” leaves the comparison point unstated.
- **Representative:** No samples, typical cases or population evidence are supplied. W1’s “whoever” and W2’s “all” have universal scope, but need not function as empirical generalizations.
- **Prescriptive:** W2 asks residents to support one another. No policy, funding decision or program prescription is supplied.

- **What exactly would the 40% measure? Applies to W3.** The neighborhood boundary, eviction measure and baseline are missing.

**Evidenced strengths**

W1 gives the welcome a clear, bounded setting: “this room tonight.” W2 turns solidarity into an intelligible action toward another family. Both observations rest on the **writer-stated aim** and the wording; neither establishes effectiveness with residents.

W3 is explicit about magnitude and timing. That makes its commitment identifiable and open to scrutiny, although specificity does not establish support.

**Evidenced weaknesses and their bases**

W3 shifts from interpersonal support to assured eviction reduction. Relative to the **writer-stated aim**, this introduces a substantial additional purpose or consequence that the greeting has not explained.

Relative to the **text-grounded reader promise**, W3 gives certainty without supplied grounds for its causal attribution or numerical precision. This is a limitation of the available support, not a finding that the prediction is false.

W2 leaves “diminished” open to interpretation. That ambiguity becomes a weakness if the intended commitment is an empirical account of collective harm; as an ethical expression of solidarity, it can serve the confirmed purpose without such an account.

**External-frame critiques**

No external evaluative norm is supplied. Requirements for policy design, funding adequacy or demonstrated persuasion should therefore not be imposed as separate grounds for criticism.

**Counter-readings, missing context and repair classes**

W2 can reasonably express shared moral concern rather than measurable harm. W3 could be ceremonial encouragement, deliberate characterization or a forecast grounded elsewhere in the fiction. Those readings remain possible, but the supplied sentence presents an unqualified prediction, and no surrounding support confirms them.

Useful repair classes include scope alignment, clarification of claim status, qualification of certainty, and—if the numerical forecast is retained—causal and measurement clarification. Removing or qualifying W3 would reduce the unsupported assurance while sacrificing its emphatic specificity. Retaining and substantiating it would preserve that specificity while expanding the greeting’s explanatory burden. Neither audience benefit nor audience cost is evidenced.
