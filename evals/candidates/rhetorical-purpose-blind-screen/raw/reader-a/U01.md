## 3. Strengths

- **Inclusive address.** The first-person plural and a shared name fit the stated aim directly. *Basis: writer aim [C1].*
- **Identity grounded in practice.** Defining keepers by "making room" means membership is earned through a modest, welcoming act rather than status or tenure. That supports identification among current neighbors. *Basis: writer aim [C1]; operation of [S1].*
- **Place-specific imagery.** The courtyard name, lamp, and doorway anchor the identity in a shared location. *Basis: writer aim.*
- **Continuity across time.** [S2] moves from tonight to tomorrow and frames the identity as something passed on rather than held once. *Basis: text promise; [S1]'s practice-based definition is carried into [S2].*

## 4. Weaknesses

- **The conditional can read as a boundary.** "We are … keepers *when* we make room" implies that people who don't make room aren't keepers. That could cut against broad shared identification. *Basis: writer aim [C1].*
  - *Counter-reading:* the "when" describes an ongoing practice that is always open to join. It invites rather than gatekeeps.
- **The imperatives are underspecified.** [S2] issues direct instructions, which implies they can be acted on. Yet the time, place, and content of the watch, and what "ready" means for the doorway, are not given. *Basis: text promise.*
  - *Counter-reading:* the intended readers may already share this knowledge, so leaving it out would be ordinary insider shorthand.
- **Scope ambiguity.** If "another neighbor" means a newcomer, the passage reaches past the confirmed "current neighbors." This would extend the stated aim rather than contradict it, but it is not covered by [C1]. *Basis: writer aim.*
  - *Counter-reading:* the phrase may simply mean whichever current neighbor arrives next.

## 7. Useful repair classes (descriptions only)

- **Logistical specification:** say where and when the watch happens and what it involves.
- **Scope clarification:** settle whether "another neighbor" means current residents, newcomers, or both.
- **Framing the conditional:** signal that the identity is open to anyone who joins, so "when" reads as an invitation rather than a test.
- **Alternate participation:** give a way to take part for people who can't attend "tonight."
- **Glossing referents:** briefly explain local terms for readers who don't know them.
