## 7. Repair classes and tradeoffs

These would be addressed through the proper routes, not by removing the finding:

- **Supply support.** This keeps the force of the claim, but requires evidence that the writer must provide.

- **Attribute the figure.** This moves responsibility to the source, but its reliability then becomes the open question.
- **Remove or separate the quantified claim from the solidarity content.** This fits the new aim, but loses the concrete stake.
