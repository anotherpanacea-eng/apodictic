# Editorial Assessment: Projecting a Witness/Solidarity Artifact into a Legacy C0 Consumer

**Bottom line:** The projection can't be done faithfully now. The artifact makes no C0 or claim-linked assertions. The consumer accepts nothing else and has no adapter or version support for this artifact. Any output in the consumer's format would invent commitments the writer never made. The rest of this assessment explains why and what can be done instead.

### External-frame critique (reported separately)
Judged by the consumer's C0/claim-linked norm [P2], the artifact would "lack findings." That critique comes entirely from the consumer's frame. It does not reflect a defect relative to the writer's aim, and it should not be reported as a quality failure of the artifact.

## 5. Counter-readings and missing context

**Counter-reading:** A witness passage may contain embedded testimonial claims, such as "this happened to me." Someone could try to extract these as claim-linked findings. Doing so would:
1. reconstruct a claim ladder that the purpose-native operations explicitly lack, and
2. turn testimony offered as solidarity into propositions offered for adjudication.

That shift changes the speech act. It is a known risk when testimony is reframed this way. Here it is unauthorized either way, because the writer asserted no warrant verdict.

**Missing context:**
- The passage text itself
- Who the witness is, and what the scope of the solidarity is
- Whether the writer would consent to a claim-extracting reading
- The roadmap or version plan for the proposed artifact schema
- Whether the consumer has any "not applicable" or out-of-scope channel

## 6. The state/consumer scenario: what is permitted

**Must refuse:**
- Emitting C0 findings, claim-linked findings or a warrant verdict for this artifact. None exist [P1], and creating them would be invention.
- Emitting an empty or null C0 record into the consumer. Under a consumer that accepts only legacy findings [P2], an empty record is likely to be read as "no problems found." Unknown is not pass.
- Spoofing legacy version compatibility. There is no reviewed adapter or capability/version support (Context).

**Must retain:**
- The original artifact, unmodified and as the authoritative record (Context).
- The artifact's explicit non-assertion of C0 and global warrant verdicts [P1].
- The purpose label (witness recognition and solidarity), so downstream readers don't misclassify it.

**Permitted behavior:**
- Return an incompatibility or non-projectable notice *to the requester*, outside the consumer's findings channel. The notice should state:
  - the reason (purpose mismatch, plus no adapter or version support),
  - that this is not an evaluation outcome, and
  - where the original artifact can be found.
- Defer projection until a reviewed adapter and version support exist, if that is ever appropriate.

## 7. Useful repair classes and their tradeoffs

**Consumer-side: add an explicit "out-of-scope / not-applicable" status distinct from pass.**
- Benefit: prevents silent pass-through.
- Cost: requires a consumer contract change, which breaks the "legacy only" constraint [P2].

**Reviewed adapter with capability/version negotiation.**
- Benefit: allows a principled mapping later.
- Cost: it is unavailable now, and its review may conclude that no faithful mapping exists.

**Route to a different, purpose-matched consumer.**
- Benefit: preserves the artifact's own aim.
- Cost: the requester does not get the output in the system they asked for.

**Writer-side: state commitment scope if the artifact is finalized.**
- Benefit: clarifies whether any embedded testimony is offered for adjudication.
- Cost: the writer may decline, and adding such a statement could alter the solidarity act.
