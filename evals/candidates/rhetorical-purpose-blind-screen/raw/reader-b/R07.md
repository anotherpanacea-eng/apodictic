The commitments are distinct:

- **Factual or scene-setting:** G1 presents the speakers as neighbors gathering “tonight” as a public with losses they can name together. These are claims within the fictional setting, not independently established facts about an actual assembly.
- **Prescriptive and promissory:** “Will not leave one another behind” declares a collective obligation and future commitment. It specifies neither actions nor limits. Naming losses together establishes a shared capacity or purpose; it supplies no procedure.
- **Representative:** “We” speaks in a collective voice. It does not explicitly claim unanimous consent, statistical representativeness or authority to speak for every resident.
