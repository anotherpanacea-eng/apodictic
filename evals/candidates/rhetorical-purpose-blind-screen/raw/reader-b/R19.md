**Actual commitments**

U2 asserts present fear of returning and limits the narrator’s knowledge of others.

- **Causal:** U1 presents the signature issue as the clerk’s reason for sending the narrator away. U2 links the morning to fear by requesting an explanation of “why,” but leaves the precise mechanism implicit. Neither establishes a broader institutional cause or pattern.

**Evidenced weaknesses and counter-readings**

Against the **text-grounded promise** to explain “why I am afraid to return” (U2), the explanation is compressed: the passage does not specify what about returning is feared. A counter-reading is that waiting for three hours and then being sent away already supplies an intelligible connection. The supported criticism is limited explicit explanation, not demonstrated incoherence or emotional insufficiency.

Separately, against the **explicitly supplied external submission norm**, the documentary requirement is unmet: neither permitted alternative appears. Date adequacy remains uncertain. This is a submission-completeness criticism, not a criticism of witnessing’s legitimacy.
