# One nonfiction counterfactual development family

Status: prospective construction protocol, frozen before prose. This is a new one-family addendum; the earlier editorial-counterfactual pilot and Protocol 2 remain unchanged. No scoring admission or positive construction disposition is promised.

Author one original synthetic practical how-to of 250–450 words. The common reader-facing brief states genre, audience and intended outcome without naming the proposed defect. Supply that brief identically to each reading. Choose a narrator-level procedural recommendation incompatible with an explicit prerequisite; avoid character unreliability, hidden author intent, naked arithmetic and artificial anti-escape clauses.

Before variants, preserve the baseline and a private brief defining exact target spans, consequence, strengths, a competing reading, conserved commitments and permitted mutation scopes. A separate author repairs only the failed recommendation while preserving the prerequisite, workload and access promises. Another independent author changes one separate substantive logistical recommendation, with an explicit benefit/tradeoff, while preserving the target spans and failure. The control may change its prespecified commitment; do not claim blanket conservation. It cannot introduce a bypass, new exception or policy change that resolves the target. Authors do not see each other's variants. Report actual edit magnitudes; these do not prove equal salience.

Freeze this protocol, then the baseline/brief, then all three final passages and common neutral prompt before readings. Preserve ordinary source files, exact dispatch records and existing whole-artifact bindings outside Git. No new custody framework. No revisions or reader replacements to reach agreement.

Three fresh instruction-separated readers each receive only the common brief and one opaque passage, without tools, condition labels, keys, siblings or earlier judgments. Ask material concerns with exact evidence, strengths, competing readings and abstention. An independent family reviewer first sees shuffled neutral variants and the brief; preserve its judgment before revealing hypotheses, ledgers and packet readings. Then assess materiality, repair resolution, control retention, conservation and confounds. Independent agreement is not human licensing.

Judgments are qualitative only. One immutable family is retained-candidate, disputed, rejected or not-evaluable; not-evaluable covers insufficient/missing evidence or failed required readings. Report proposed = retained + disputed + rejected + not-evaluable and all three reading outcomes, including failures. No reconstruction/REWORK path, numeric diagnosis, updating run, benchmark score or comparison. Standalone readings cannot establish withdrawal behavior. All descendants remain DEV; no untouched holdout, corpus-wide originality, decontamination, naturalistic-fiction validity or ground-truth promotion is claimed. Runtime assignment and unavailable telemetry are recorded honestly; no inferred model identity.

Public delivery contains protocol and aggregate observations only. Prose, keys, ledgers, complete judgments and private paths remain outside Git. Independent exact-head source and Fleet review precede an unarmed draft; Claude review/integration remain owed. Provider spend, production calls, training, GPU, deployment and merge authorization are separate gates.
