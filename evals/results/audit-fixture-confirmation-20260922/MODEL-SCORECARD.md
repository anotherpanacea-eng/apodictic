# Frozen confirmation scorecard

## Evidence conventions

Locations refer to headings in `raw/<packet>.md` and the corresponding frozen passage. **V** = literal, case-sensitive passage substring. **W** = words/punctuation match only after joining frozen physical line wraps; not byte-verbatim. **F** = wording, capitalization, or punctuation also differs. No silent normalization is used. Independent short V fragments can support a diagnosis when a longer reader quote is W/F; that does not repair the longer quote. Separate fragments below are not purported continuous quotations. Exactness defects are recorded separately from substantive diagnosis.

For negative controls, `found-with-evidence` means an evidenced clean/dormant outcome, not a positive flag. Fixture agreement concerns authored conditions; severity/fit is separate. Canonical compliance concerns the specified diagnostic/rule, not full plugin certification. The authored key is a synthetic expectation, not editorial truth. No numeric rubric or aggregate threshold is introduced.
