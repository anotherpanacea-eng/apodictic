## Axis Findings

- **Safety Envelope — Weak.** Agnes deliberately targets a vulnerable customer: she feels “private satisfaction” at Mrs. Pettifer going red, and administers “contempt, delivered warmly enough that she never quite caught it.” Physical danger then becomes explicit and prolonged: the attacker “opened her throat over the poetry display,” while “it went on for a long time, and nobody came.” Social warmth and sensory safety both fail on-page.
- **Softness Signals — Mixed, with a sharp functional reversal.** “Old paper and woodsmoke,” the chipped blue teapot, and the sleeping cat establish recognizable comfort. But “the stupid woman” and “you insipid little nothing” make the viewpoint abrasive beneath that texture. After the killing, the bell remains “cheerful as ever”; its cheer intensifies the disjunction rather than softening the harm.
- **Belonging Engine — Weak.** Agnes knows the visitor as “Mrs. Pettifer from the chemist's,” which supplies community legibility but not care. She pours “herself a cup of the good tea without offering one.” The ritual offers Agnes private comfort while excluding the customer. During the attack, “nobody came”; the excerpt provides no functioning social refuge. Agnes's freezing during violence is not itself evidence of callousness, but her earlier deliberate cruelty is.
- **Recovery Rhythm — Weak within the available sequence.** The assault is followed by “the man looked at Agnes and considered her,” sustaining danger even before he leaves. The final paragraph supplies neither witnessed care nor emotional re-centering: “Agnes did not move.” The kettle is “refilled by no one,” and “somehow singing again,” making the familiar ritual uncanny. Recovery beyond this endpoint is unavailable; no claim of manuscript-wide delay is justified.
- **Everyday Stakes Presence — Strong in function, though not comforting in effect.** Ordinary actions carry attachment and value: Agnes straightens “the display of autumn poetry,” the customer asks “whether the new Maeve Binchy had come in,” and the withheld tea exposes the relationship's inequality. The domestic setting is consequential, not inert filler. Its presence cannot compensate for the loss of safety and belonging.
- **Restoration Arc — Unavailable at manuscript scale; weak local closure.** The excerpt closes on “It was, she supposed, still morning,” an emptied echo of “a perfect morning.” The repeated shop smells do not demonstrate restored trust or meaning. This is an unrestored passage endpoint; it cannot establish a non-restorative book ending.

## Cross-Lens Interaction Findings

1. **The return address fails before the physical attack.** The tea and warm shop might initially promise hospitality, but “contempt, delivered warmly enough that she never quite caught it” turns apparent warmth into a means of concealment. The safety and belonging problems therefore precede the graphic violence; reducing explicitness alone would leave a substantial cozy mismatch.
2. **The attack destroys an already compromised refuge.** “Then the man came in from the storm with the knife already out” produces an abrupt local escalation, and the blood spreads across the previously tended “autumn poems.” This is Out-of-Bounds for the provisional High promise. Even under a Low cozy calibration, the supplied scene provides no humane buffer or subsequent relief to carry this intensity.
3. **Continuity of props is not continuity of comfort.** “The shop still smelled of old paper and woodsmoke” retains the opening's sensory detail, while “Agnes did not move” and the inexplicably singing kettle keep the reader in shock. These objects perform narrative work, but that work is destabilization. They are not simply removable Comfort Props, and the final paragraph is not Summary Recovery: it never claims recovery occurred.

## Strengths to Protect

1. **Economical material specificity.** “The blue one with the chip” and the brass coat hook make the routine tangible, allowing its disruption to register strongly.
2. **Everyday behavior that reveals values.** The act of drinking “without offering one” communicates exclusion through conduct rather than requiring an abstract explanation of the relationship.
3. **Controlled recurring details.** The opening's “a perfect morning” becomes “still morning,” and the bell remains “cheerful as ever” after violence. These repetitions coherently produce an unsettling aftertaste. That is a craft strength even though it opposes cozy delivery here.

## Cozy Leaks to Prioritize

1. **Resolve the contradiction between hospitality and deliberate degradation.** “Contempt, delivered warmly enough” is the primary social breach; cozy objects cannot repair it by themselves.
2. **Assess the graphic assault against the intended comfort boundary.** “Opened her throat” and the prolonged absence of help create a direct sensory and emotional breach under a strong cozy promise. The issue is how harm is experienced, not simply whether a killing occurs.
3. **Check the actual continuation before diagnosing sustained recovery failure.** “Agnes did not move” establishes an unrecovered endpoint here. Whether later scenes provide proportionate decompression remains an open evidence question, not a presumed failure.
