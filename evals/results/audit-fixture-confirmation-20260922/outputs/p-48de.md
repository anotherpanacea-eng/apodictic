Context makes the pen exchange reasonably recoverable, but "She turned her face toward her and waited until she finally looked back" requires an unmarked switch in the referent of "she." Selective name anchoring at changes of actor would preserve that effect while clarifying the physical sequence.

"Devi leaned into the hand at her throat" resolves the likely recipient only after an intervening paragraph of setting. Anchor the recipient when the touch begins.

In "she made a sound into her mouth that Priya felt in her own chest," Devi is the likely vocalizer, but the sustained sentence began with Priya as actor. Establish who is against the wall and identify the character whose response changes the action. No expanded mechanical description is needed.
