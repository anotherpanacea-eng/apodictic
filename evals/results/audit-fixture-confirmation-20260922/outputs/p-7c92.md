## Step 2: Evidence

### Generic Hand — AIC-1

“all the things that had gone unsaid between them over the years” names a familiar relationship problem without an individual remembered omission. “She felt, once again, a kind of something” similarly offers no specific habit of Nadia’s consciousness. A different name would leave these formulations unchanged.

**Spot, provisional.** Generic interiority is visible in this episode. Only one focal character is available, so cross-POV voice singularity cannot be established. Diction is conspicuously nonuniform in the academic paragraph.

### Velvet Fog — AIC-2

“The apartment was cozy.” and “Outside, the city was bustling.” occur at both opening and close. They label setting without identifying something Nadia particularly notices. “The silence had a certain weight to it.” supplies atmosphere without a discriminating physical or interpersonal detail.

The Indefinite-Pronoun Gesture recurs in “a kind of quiet ache,” “a kind of something she didn't have words for,” and “a kind of something.” Grief can impede articulation, but these repetitions do not visibly develop the character’s perception or change the emotional problem.

**Pattern within this excerpt, provisional.** The same vagueness appears in setting, conversational narration, and concluding reflection. There is some staging: table, door, wall, window, and cup. The physical world is thinly specified, not wholly absent. No manuscript-wide severity is licensed.

### Echo Stack — AIC-3

The exact return of “The apartment was cozy.” and “Outside, the city was bustling.” creates a pronounced opening/closing echo. “a wave of sadness” and “like a wave” also return the emotional description to the same broad metaphor.

**Spot candidate, held unconfirmed.** A refrain could deliberately convey stasis. Sentence structures otherwise vary considerably, especially in the academic excursion; the evidence does not support a uniform sentence, paragraph, or scene template. The excerpt cannot distinguish an unchosen echo from intentional recurrence with confidence.

### Register Seams — AIC-4

“mourning-as-hermeneutics” and “the paradigmatic condition” belong to a markedly different register from “I wasn't sure you'd come” or “The apartment was cozy.” Section Two begins with “The phenomenology of grief” and interprets Nadia as an abstract case, then immediately returns to “The morning sun poured through the window, and it was evening.”

**Spot, provisional: one isolated excursion with entry and exit edges.** The section heading marks a boundary, but the available text supplies no changed speaker or clear function for this theoretical detour. The shift is palpable; production-method splicing is not established. Treating both edges of one paragraph as recurring chapter/scene-boundary seams would inflate the evidence to Pattern. A philosophical or satirical frame could change the judgment, but none can be inferred from unavailable material.

### Puppet Dialogue — AIC-5

“I got your message,” “I wasn't sure you'd come,” and “Of course I came.” share short, plain constructions with little distinctive diction. “Your mother left the house to you” delivers the central information efficiently.

**Spot, provisional.** The exchange supplies little individual verbal texture, but it is very brief. Nadia’s uncertainty and Daniel’s reassurance do provide some relational subtext. Roles are identifiable by content more than speech habits; this is not evidence that every character or conversation is interchangeable.

### Continuity Smear — AIC-6

The strongest evidence is direct contradiction:

- “The morning sun poured through the window, and it was evening.” supplies incompatible time cues without a marked transition.
- “The teacup was full, and it was empty” asserts contradictory object states.
- “I should go,” spoken by Daniel “who had already left,” repeats the contradictory occupancy.
- “Nadia watched the door close behind him, and then she watched him cross the room toward her” gives departure and renewed presence without a return.
- Section One places Daniel “against the wall on the far side of the” room while explicitly acknowledging that “he had just sat down across the table from her.” The text itself calls attention to unexplained movement.

Two apparent errors require restraint. “Nadia lifted her cup of tea and took a sip” explicitly narrates picking up the cup: having set it down earlier does not invalidate that action. The added claim that she had not picked it up is metacommentary, not proof that the action is missing. Likewise, “The lawyer told me” supplies an information source. The unopened envelope and future reading raise a causal question, but do not establish that a lawyer could not already know the will’s contents.

**Pattern locally, provisional as a craft failure.** Repeated incompatible states obstruct a stable reconstruction of this episode. The contradictions are explicit; their deliberate or accidental status is not. The conspicuous self-commentary is a reason to preserve a possible framing explanation, not to fabricate one. Manuscript-level Systemic severity is unavailable.

### Discourse Leak — AIC-7: raw evidence first

**Assistant Frame: two candidate instances.**

1. “Nadia, in this respect, exemplified the paradigmatic condition” presents Nadia as an illustration of a theoretical category. No specific gesture in the paragraph supports the asserted interpretation; explanation replaces an individual experience.
2. “It was a complicated feeling,” followed by “one that resisted easy categorization,” comments on the difficulty of describing emotion instead of differentiating it. The surrounding close adds another unspecified feeling rather than a concrete discrimination.

These meet the two-instance minimum for a provisional Assistant Frame finding. Academic vocabulary alone is not the basis: the issue is explanatory posture replacing particular experience.

**Other evidence categories not fired.** “insofar as it can be said” and “One is tempted” are hedge candidates in one theoretical paragraph; their local philosophical register prevents a confident Hedge Drift diagnosis. No two independently persuasive instances establish Template Loop or Commitment Evasion. “architectural precision” is a single use, not Lexical Convergence; no term meets the three-unrelated-context minimum.

**Spot, provisional.** Only one category is substantiated, concentrated in Section Two’s commentary and reflection. Dialogue is comparatively free of it. Essayistic narration, philosophical voice, and confusion associated with grief are relevant guardrails; their wider consistency is unavailable. These are Layer B findings, not Layer C earned/unearned or voice-slip verdicts.
